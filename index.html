How results are produced. Every month the pipeline (1) downloads Open Ownership's worldwide map to find countries with a live, full-economy BO register and its legal access rules; (2) visits each register's website with a headless browser and follows links to the BO search; (3) answers the four objective criteria - open to the public, anonymous access, free of charge, access without a legitimate-interest test - from that evidence with transparent rules and an AI reading of the pages whose quotes are checked against the page text; (4) scores ease of access and tracks stability from monthly uptime. Where evidence is missing the value is Unknown and the country is listed for a human check.
Sources. Open Ownership worldwide map (openownership.org); national BO registry websites; country list, BO law references, regions and FCS status maintained by the project team. Automated results can be wrong - confirm on the registry website before relying on them.
